Right and wrong · Beliefs · Time

Jareef Martuza

Jareef Martuza is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Marketing at the Norwegian School of Economics. He studies decision-making by focusing on how people think about right and wrong, accuracy and bias in common beliefs, and how we all think about time. His research has been published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, and Nature Human Behaviour.
