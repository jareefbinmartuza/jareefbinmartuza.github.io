Jareef Martuza

Jareef Martuza is a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Marketing at the Norwegian School of Economics. He researches who people are more likely to cheat, how accurate our beliefs are, and how we think about time. His research has been published in the Journal of Experimental Social Psychology, Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, and Nature Human Behaviour.
